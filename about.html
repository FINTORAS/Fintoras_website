Small teams deserve to know which work pays

Our mission is to help small teams see their true project profitability and make smarter decisions.

The problem

Small teams are drowning in tool complexity. They juggle project management software, accounting systems, spreadsheets and invoicing tools, all disconnected.

The result is that they can't see which projects actually make money. They overprice some projects and underprice others. They don't know whether they're efficient or wasting time. They make decisions on gut feeling instead of data.

Meanwhile, large enterprises have million-dollar software suites and finance teams. The playing field isn't level.

The insight. Small teams don't need a complex enterprise tool. They need one integrated platform that combines project management with financial intelligence.

Our mission

Help small teams unlock project profitability by giving them the unified platform they deserve: project management, financial tracking and AI-powered insight in one place.

Our vision

A future where every team, whatever its size, has the same level of financial intelligence and project insight that Fortune 500 companies use to thrive.

Sunil Uthukota is the founder and CEO of Fintoras. His background is in industrial technology and management, and he spent nearly four years in project management and financial analysis, working on construction and engineering projects valued at $45 million.

In that work, his research and recommendations to management brought in more than $6 million in profit. Fintoras is built to put that kind of analysis in the hands of small teams that don't have an analyst of their own.

He holds two master's degrees and is a certified Project Management Professional (PMP).
