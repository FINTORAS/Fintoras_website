We're Building the Future of Project Management

A mission to help small teams see their true project profitability and make smarter decisions.

The Problem

Small teams are drowning in tool complexity. They juggle project management software, accounting systems, spreadsheets, and invoicing tools—all disconnected.

The result? They can't see which projects actually make money. They overprice some projects and underprice others. They don't know if they're efficient or wasting time. They make decisions based on gut feeling instead of data.

Meanwhile, large enterprises have million-dollar software suites and finance teams. The playing field isn't level.

💡 The Insight

Small teams don't need a complex enterprise tool. They need one integrated platform that combines project management with financial intelligence—powered by AI.

Our Mission

Empower small teams to unlock project profitability by providing the unified intelligence platform they deserve—combining project management, financial tracking, and AI-powered insights in one place.

Our Vision

A future where every team, regardless of size, has access to the same level of financial intelligence and project insights that Fortune 500 companies use to thrive.

Our Story

2023

The Discovery

Our founder worked with a 15-person creative agency struggling to track project profitability. They were using 5 different tools and still couldn't answer a simple question: "Which projects make us money?"

2024

The Opportunity

We realized this wasn't a unique problem. Hundreds of thousands of small teams face the same challenge. The market had project management tools and accounting tools, but no platform combining both with AI intelligence.

2025

FINTORAS is Born

We started building FINTORAS with a simple vision: create the platform we wished existed. One that's powerful yet simple, affordable yet enterprise-grade, and designed specifically for small teams.
