Frequently Asked Questions

How can I get early access?
Join our waitlist and we'll notify you as soon as early access opens. Early access users often get special perks and lifetime discounts.
Do you offer demos?
Yes! During our closed beta, we offer personalized demos for qualified teams. Fill out our contact form and select "Request a Demo."
Can I import my existing project data?
Absolutely. We support bulk imports from Monday.com, Asana, and CSV files. Our team can help with migration during onboarding.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, AmEx) and bank transfers for Enterprise plans. All payments are processed securely through Stripe.
Is my financial data secure?
Security is our top priority. We use enterprise-grade encryption, regular security audits, and comply with SOC 2 standards. Read our full security documentation on our privacy page.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes, cancel anytime with no penalty. Your data remains accessible for 30 days after cancellation, giving you time to export or migrate.
Do you have an API?
Yes! Professional and Enterprise plans include API access for custom integrations. Documentation is available in the dashboard.
What's your uptime guarantee?
We maintain 99.9% uptime SLA on all paid plans. Check our status page for real-time system status.
