Common questions

How do I get early access?

Join the waitlist and we'll email you when early access opens.

Can I see a demo?

While the product is in development we are showing it to a small number of teams. Choose Request a demo in the form and tell us a little about yours.

Do I have to replace the tools I use today?

No. CSV import and an API are both planned for launch, so you can bring projects, hours and costs in from the tools you already use. Tell us which ones and we'll take it into account.

How will my financial data be protected?

Customer data will be encrypted in transit and at rest. The security page sets out what is decided and what is still open.

Will there be an API?

Yes. API access is planned for launch on the Professional plan and above.

What will it cost?

The planned prices are on the pricing page, along with answers about trials, billing and cancelling.
