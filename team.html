Who is building Fintoras

A small company on a mission to change how small teams manage projects and finances. We plan to launch by the end of Q1 2027.

SU

Sunil Uthukota

Founder and CEO

  • PMP certified
  • Two master's degrees

sunil@fintoras.com

Sunil Uthukota is the founder and CEO of Fintoras. His background is in industrial technology and management, and he spent nearly four years in project management and financial analysis, working on $15 million projects.

In that work, his research and recommendations to management brought in more than $6 million in profit. Fintoras is built to put that kind of analysis in the hands of small teams that don't have an analyst of their own.

He holds two master's degrees and is a certified Project Management Professional (PMP).

His vision for Fintoras goes beyond software. He is committed to making financial intelligence available to teams of every size, and to building products that are powerful enough for professionals yet simple enough for anyone to use.
