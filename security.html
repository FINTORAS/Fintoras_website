Security and your data

What is true today, what is committed for launch, and what is still undecided. We would rather show you the gaps than hide them. Last updated 9 October 2026.

This website today

Fintoras the product has not launched, so the only data we hold is what you send through this site.

  • Served over HTTPS

    Every page on fintoras.com is delivered over an encrypted connection.

  • No cookies or trackers

    The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts.

  • Waitlist and contact forms

    What you enter is stored by Netlify, our website host, on our behalf. We use it only to contact you about Fintoras.

  • The calculator

    It runs entirely in your browser. The figures you type are never sent to us.

Committed for launch

Launch is planned by the end of Q1 2027.

  • Encryption

    Customer data will be encrypted in transit and at rest.

  • Testing before launch

    We are testing the AI on our own proprietary data and with a small group of volunteer companies before anyone relies on it.

Not decided yet

These are open. We will state our position on each one on this page before the first customer account opens.

  • Hosting provider and data location

    Not chosen yet. We will name the provider and the region where data is stored.

  • Who can see cost rates

    Per-person cost rates are sensitive. We will set out how access to them is controlled inside a customer's account.

  • Backups, export and deletion

    How often data is backed up, and how you take your data out or have it deleted.

  • Independent audit

    Whether and when a third-party security audit will be carried out.
