How the numbers are worked out

Fintoras is being built to run this sum for every project, every day, from the hours and costs your team already records.

  • Projected hours

    Hours logged so far, divided by the share of the work that is done. 260 hours at 50% complete projects to 520.

  • Projected cost

    Projected hours times your cost per hour, plus other costs.

  • Margin

    Fee minus projected cost, as a share of the fee.

  • Break-even

    Fee minus other costs, divided by cost per hour. Past this many hours, the project loses money.
