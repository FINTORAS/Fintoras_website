Everything between the task and the margin

Six parts that share one set of numbers. Fintoras is still in development, so this page describes what we are building for launch.

Project management

Organise the work in one place, in the view each person prefers.

  • Tasks

    Create projects, break them into tasks and assign them to people.

  • Board, timeline and list

    Kanban boards, Gantt charts, a timeline view and a plain list.

  • Custom fields

    Add priority, status, tags and any other property your team uses.

  • Collaboration

    Comments, file attachments, mentions and an activity feed on every task.

  • Deadlines

    Set due dates and milestones, and get alerted before they slip.

  • Automation

    Update tasks automatically from triggers and rules you define.

The kind of flag Fintoras raises. Sample data.

  • Unusual activity

    Flags spending spikes, timeline slips and scope changes.

  • Profit forecast

    Projects each job's final margin from its pace so far.

  • Suggestions

    Proposes what to change: trim scope, reprice, or move people.

  • Budget alerts

    Tells you when a project starts heading off track, not after.

  • Trends

    Uses past projects to improve estimates and pricing on the next one.

  • Questions in plain language

    Ask about your projects and get an answer drawn from your own data.
