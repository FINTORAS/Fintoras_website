Powerful Features Built for Project Success

Everything you need to manage projects, track finances, and make data-driven decisions in one unified platform.

Project Management

Organize all your work in one place with intuitive project management tools.

  • 📋 Task Management: Create projects, break them into tasks, and assign to team members
  • 📈 Multiple Views: Kanban boards, Gantt charts, timeline view, and list view
  • 🏷️ Custom Fields: Add priority, status, custom tags, and properties
  • 👥 Team Collaboration: Comments, file attachments, @mentions, activity feed
  • 📅 Deadline Management: Set deadlines, get alerts, track milestones
  • ♻️ Workflow Automation: Auto-update tasks based on triggers and rules

Financial Tracking

Track every penny spent on your projects and see true profitability in real-time.

  • 💵 Budget Creation: Set project budgets by task, team member, or expense category
  • 📝 Expense Logging: Record time costs, materials, subcontractors, and overhead
  • 📊 Budget vs. Actual: Real-time comparison showing variance and burn rate
  • 💹 Profit Margins: Automatic calculation of profit margins per project
  • 🎯 Cost Allocation: Allocate shared costs across projects intelligently
  • 💱 Multi-currency: Support for multiple currencies and automatic conversion
🤖

AI-Powered Insights

Leverage artificial intelligence to uncover opportunities and prevent problems before they happen.

  • ⚠️ Anomaly Detection: AI flags unusual spending, timeline slips, or scope changes
  • 📈 Profit Forecasting: ML-powered predictions of final project profitability
  • 🎯 Smart Recommendations: AI suggests optimizations (reduce scope, adjust pricing, reallocate resources)
  • 🚨 Budget Alerts: Proactive notifications when projects are going off track
  • 📊 Trend Analysis: Historical patterns to improve estimates and pricing
  • 💡 Insights Bot: Ask questions in natural language, get instant analysis

Time Tracking

Know the true cost of every project with integrated time tracking tied directly to tasks.

  • ⏱️ Time Entries: Log hours directly on tasks with descriptions and breaks
  • ⏱️ Timer: Start/stop timer for real-time tracking during work
  • 💼 Team Capacity: See how time is allocated across projects and team members
  • ⚠️ Overtime Alerts: Get notified when tasks exceed estimated hours
  • 📊 Utilization Reports: Dashboard showing team member utilization and billable hours
  • 💰 Rate Management: Set hourly rates per person and project type
📈

Intelligent Reporting

Get comprehensive insights with pre-built reports and customizable dashboards.

  • 📊 Profitability Report: Detailed breakdown of revenue, costs, and margins per project
  • 👥 Team Utilization: See how efficiently team members are allocated
  • 💹 Financial Summary: Monthly P&L, cash flow, and trend analysis
  • 🎯 Custom Dashboards: Build your own views with drag-and-drop widgets
  • 📧 Scheduled Reports: Auto-email reports on daily, weekly, or monthly basis
  • 📥 Export & Share: Download as PDF, Excel, or share via link
