On time is not the same as profitable

Take the Kestrel app from the sample above. It shipped on the agreed date. Every weekly status report said on track. The client was pleased.

It also took 380 hours more than planned. Two change requests added 210 of them and were never billed. Rework after late feedback added the other 170. Contractor and licence costs ran over too.

Nobody added it up until the job was finished, because the hours lived in one tool, the costs in another and the fee in a third. Fintoras keeps them together and keeps this sum up to date as hours and costs come in.

From task to margin in four steps

Each step feeds the next, so the margin at the end is never a separate report someone has to build.

  1. Plan the work

    Set up the project, break it into tasks and give it a budget. See it as a board, a list or a timeline.

  2. Log the time

    People track hours against the tasks they are already working on, with a timer or by hand.

  3. Cost it as you go

    Every hour is priced at that person's cost rate. Expenses and contractor bills land on the same project.

  4. Read the margin

    Fee minus cost for each project, updated as the work happens and not at month end.

See everything it does

It warns you while there is still margin to save

Fintoras checks every active project against its plan. When the numbers drift, it says what moved and by how much.

  • Spend running ahead of progress

    Compares budget used with work completed, and projects where the job will finish if nothing changes.

  • Hours over estimate

    Flags a task once logged time passes its estimate, and shows what that does to the project margin.

  • Work nobody is billing

    Picks out tasks added after the budget was agreed that have no change order behind them.

Made for small teams who sell their time

A good fit if

  • You run an agency, a studio, a consultancy or a development shop.

  • You bill by the project or on retainer.

  • Your tasks live in one tool and your numbers live in a spreadsheet.

Probably not for you if

  • You need full accounting or payroll. Fintoras is planned to connect to QuickBooks, not replace it.

  • You are a large firm with a finance department and an enterprise suite already in place.

  • Your team builds its own product and never bills a client.
