Pricing questions

Something else? Write to hello@fintoras.com.

When does Fintoras launch?

We plan to go live by the end of the first quarter of 2027. People on the waitlist will hear first, along with any change to these prices.

Will there be a free trial?

That's the plan: 14 days on any plan, with no card needed to start.

Is there a discount for paying yearly?

Yes, 20%. Starter comes to $950 a year instead of $1,188, and Professional to $2,870 instead of $3,588.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes, with no penalty. We plan to keep your data available for 30 days after you cancel so you can export it.

What if my team outgrows a plan?

Move up to the next plan. If you are only just over a limit, get in touch before you do and we will work something out.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Enterprise customers can add onboarding and data migration help, which is priced separately.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, at any time. An upgrade is charged pro rata for the rest of the billing period. A downgrade is credited against your next bill.

How will I be able to pay?

By major credit or debit card. Enterprise customers will also be able to pay by bank transfer.

Is there a discount for nonprofits?

We plan to offer nonprofits and schools 50% off. Email hello@fintoras.com and we'll note it against your waitlist entry.

Do you charge per project?

No. The price depends on the plan, not on how many projects you run inside it.
